  • Advertising information, such as information related to advertisements shown or interacted with.
  • App and user interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video/ad interactions.

2. Local Game Data

ZenWord Puzzle may store gameplay information locally on your Android device so that the game can remember your progress and preferences.

This may include:

  • Completed levels and puzzle progress.
  • Stars, coins, rewards, and other in-game progress.
  • Words discovered and vocabulary progress.
  • Daily puzzle and streak progress.
  • Selected themes and game preferences.
  • Sound and haptic preferences.

Local gameplay data is used to provide the game's functionality and is not intended by Keshav Studios to identify you personally.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

ZenWord Puzzle does not require an account for ordinary gameplay, so Keshav Studios does not maintain a user account database for core game progress.

Gameplay information stored locally generally remains on your device until you remove the app's data, clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes it.

You can normally delete locally stored game data through your Android device's app settings or by uninstalling ZenWord Puzzle. The exact steps may differ by Android device and version.

Data handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own retention, deletion, privacy, and account/device-control mechanisms. Please consult Google's Privacy Policy for Google's practices.
